FAQs for Upmind
1. General Questions
Q: What is Upmind?
A: Upmind is a habit tracker app designed to help you build better habits, track your progress, and achieve your goals.

Q: Is Upmind free to use?
A: Upmind is free to use with a limited number of habits. You can unlock additional features and habits by subscribing to Upmind Pro.

2. Using the App
Q: How do I create a new habit?
A: You have 2 options, Option 1: Go to the "Main" screen where you see a list of your created habits under which there is a button named "Create a new habit". Option 2: In the "Main" screen, at the very right bottom, there is a plus button select a category, and follow the prompts to customize your habit.

Q: Can I set reminders for my habits?
A: Yes! You can set reminders in the habit settings or enable morning, afternoon, and evening reminders in the app settings.

Q: How do I delete a habit?
A: To delete a habit, swipe left on the habit in your list and confirm the deletion.

Q: Can I use Upmind offline?
A: Yes! Upmind is offline-first. Your data syncs automatically when you reconnect to the internet.

3. Subscription and Premium Features
Q: What’s included in Upmind Pro?
A: Upmind Pro includes unlimited habits, advanced habit analytics, and more.

Q: How do I subscribe to Upmind Pro?
A: Go to Settings > Subscription and select your preferred plan.

Q: Can I cancel my subscription?
A: Yes, you can cancel your subscription anytime through the App Store or Google Play Store settings.

Q: How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
A: Go to Settings > Restore Purchases and log in with the same account you used to subscribe.

4. Data & Sync
Q: How do I sync my data across devices?
A: Log in with the same account on each device to sync your data automatically.

Q: Can I back up my data?
A: Yes, your data is automatically backed up to our secure servers if you’re logged in.

Q: What happens if I uninstall the app?
A: Your data will remain safe if you've synced it to the cloud. Just log in again after reinstalling.

5. Troubleshooting
Q: I’m not receiving notifications. What should I do?
A: Check if notifications are enabled for Upmind in your device settings. Ensure reminder times are set in the app.

Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
A: Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen and follow the instructions to reset your password.

Q: My app crashed. What should I do?
A: Restart the app and check for updates. If the issue persists, contact support at contact@Upmind.com.

6. Contact and Support
Q: How can I contact support?
A: You can email us at contact@Upmind.com or use the "Contact Us" option in the app settings.

Q: How do I provide feedback or suggest a new feature?
A: We love hearing from our users! Use the "Feedback" option in Settings or email us.
